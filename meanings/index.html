What does 520 mean in Chinese?

520 (wǔ èr líng) sounds like 我爱你 'I love you'. May 20 is an unofficial Valentine's Day in China.

What does 1314 mean?

一生一世 'one life, one lifetime' — forever. 5201314 means 'I love you forever'.
